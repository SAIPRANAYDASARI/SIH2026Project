On what grounds can BIS cancel a licence?
Verified against 3 BIS documents HYBRID · BM25 + BGE-M3

Under Regulation 11 of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018, the Bureau may cancel a licence after giving not less than twenty-one days' notice, on two grounds S1

(a) the licensee fails to comply with any of the conditions of the licence;
(b) the licence has been issued in error.

A licence may also be cancelled without further notice where it has been under suspension for more than one year, or where the licensee requests cancellation S1

The twenty-one day notice is a procedural requirement, not an independent ground for cancellation. Suspension, stop-marking and prosecution are separate actions S2
