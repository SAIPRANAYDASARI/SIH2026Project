> On what grounds can BIS cancel a licence?
✓ GROUNDED 3 sources | gpt-oss-20b | 18.4s

Under Regulation 11 of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018, the Bureau may cancel a licence after not less than twenty-one days' notice, on two grounds S1

(a)the licensee fails to comply with any of the conditions of the licence;
(b)the licence has been issued in error.
The twenty-one day notice is a procedural requirement, not an independent ground. S2
